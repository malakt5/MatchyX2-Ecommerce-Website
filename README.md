# MatchyX2 — E-commerce Website

## 🛍️ Overview

**MatchyX2** is an e-commerce website designed around the concept of **matching products for couples, friends, and families**.

The platform combines matching outfits and accessories with personalized gift ideas, providing customers with a shopping experience centered around connection, style, and personalization.

The project was developed using **WordPress and WooCommerce**, with a focus on e-commerce functionality, product presentation, and user experience.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🛒 **E-commerce functionality** — Product browsing, cart, and checkout
* 👕 **Matching products** — Outfits and accessories designed for couples, friends, and families
* 🎁 **Personalized gifts** — Gift ideas with a personalization-oriented concept
* 🏷️ **Product catalog** — Organized presentation of available products
* 🔎 **Product exploration** — Easy navigation through different product categories
* 💻 **Responsive website interface** — Designed for a smooth and accessible shopping experience
* ⚙️ **WooCommerce integration** — E-commerce management and shopping functionality

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of MatchyX2 was to create a functional e-commerce platform around a specific market concept: **matching products and personalized gifts for people who want to express their connection through coordinated items**.

The project provided practical experience in:

* WordPress website development
* WooCommerce e-commerce configuration
* Product and category management
* Website customization
* User interface and user experience
* E-commerce structure and navigation
